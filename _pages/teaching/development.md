## UA AI Experience

I completed the UA AI Experience program at The University of Alabama. The program introduced a practical framework for responsible AI use: Discern, Direct, Double-check, and Defend. It reinforced my view that AI tools can support learning, but their value depends on human judgment, careful verification, and responsible use. As a computing educator, I want students to learn when to use these tools, how to question their outputs, and how to take responsibility for their work.
